About Eth.ink Studio

Eth.ink Studio is a premier creative space located at Beau Vallon, Seychelles. We specialize in professional body art, precision body piercings, fine art gallery showcases, custom digital illustrations, and large-scale environmental mural installations. Visit our studio to explore our coastal store, curated local artisan souvenirs, and custom in-house apparel and jewellery lines.

We strive to keep all of our supplies and practices environmentally friendly, you can fully trust that with us you are contributing to the wellness and conservation of the environment.

- In-House Apparel & Jewellery

Eth.ink Studio is the official home and retail outlet for Bigarade, our custom apparel and handcrafted jewellery brand. Inspired by coastal island aesthetics and modern design, our original apparel and custom jewellery collections are available exclusively inside the studio.

What We Offer

Tattoos & Body Piercings

Custom tattoo artwork, body piercing services, strict studio hygiene standards, and implant-grade titanium body jewellery.

Bigarade Apparel & Jewellery

Exclusive in-house fashion line, printed apparel, custom jewellery designs, and curated beach lifestyle goods.

Art Gallery & Souvenirs

Fine art exhibitions, original visual canvas displays, handcrafted coastal items, and authentic local Seychellois souvenirs.

Illustrations & Murals

Professional digital illustration, graphic design services, comic layouts, and commercial or environmental mural work.
